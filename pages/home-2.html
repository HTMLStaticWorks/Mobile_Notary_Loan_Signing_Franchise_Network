Streamlined Workflow

The Loan Signing Journey

A modern 6-phase execution framework designed for speed, accuracy, and title peace of mind.

01

Order Dispatch

Order received from lender or title officer. Smart dispatch engine matches nearest verified signing agent.

02

Signer Confirmation

Agent contacts signers immediately to verify appointment time, location, and valid government ID requirements.

03

Dual-Tray Prep

Full closing package printed on dual-tray laser printers and audited for special lender instructions.

04

Guided Signing

Agent conducts on-site or RON session, ensuring every signature, date, and notarization is 100% accurate.

05

Rapid Scan-Backs

Critical closing pages uploaded immediately via mobile encrypted scanner for title officer funding approval.

06

Tracked Shipping

Original signed documents dropped at FedEx/UPS before final cutoff with automated tracking updates.
