Our Evolution

From a Local Signing Agency to a Nationwide Franchise Network

Founded in 2012, NotaryFranchise was created to solve a persistent industry problem: inconsistent quality and unreliable scheduling during mortgage loan closings. We built a standardized compliance platform and expanded into a turnkey franchise network.
